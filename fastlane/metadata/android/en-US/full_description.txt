A mobile client for the Symbifox modules for Odoo. The email and SMS of your
instance arrive in a single inbox, with sorting, filing and routing done on the
server rather than on the phone.

Features:

* Unified email and SMS inbox, in two tabs
* Reply, forward and compose, with attachments
* Archive, snooze, and a five-second undo
* Route a message to an Odoo record, or create a task, ticket, lead, invoice or
  expense straight from an email
* Push notifications over UnifiedPush: no Google Play Services, no Firebase
* Configurable swipe gestures and quick-action buttons
* Offline cache
* Brand colours taken from the Odoo instance it connects to

The app asks for your Odoo instance address on first launch. It never sees your
password: sign-in goes through Odoo's own web login, which returns a single-use
code.

This is a client. It requires the bf_email_management and bf_sms_archive Odoo
modules, which are proprietary (Business Source License 1.1).
