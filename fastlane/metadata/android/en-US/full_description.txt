Your Odoo working day on the phone: meetings, deadlines, email and texts from
your instance in a single app.

Mobile client for the Symbifox modules. Sorting, filing and routing happen on
the server rather than on the phone, so what you see here is what your desk
sees, at the same moment.

Features:

* Calendar in day, week or list view, in your Odoo calendar's own colours, with
  each meeting's agenda and minutes
* Snooze and dismiss a reminder, confirm attendance, create a meeting
* Deadlines: tags, stage, priority, completion and creation in place
* Unified email and SMS inbox, in two tabs
* Reply, forward, compose, with attachments
* Archive, snooze, and a five-second undo
* Route a message to an Odoo record, create a task, ticket, lead, invoice or
  expense from an email
* Assistant and softphone, when the instance offers them
* UnifiedPush notifications: no Google Play Services, no Firebase
* Configurable swipe gestures and quick actions
* Offline cache
* Light, dark or system theme, in the connected instance's brand colours

The app asks for your instance address on first launch. It never sees your
password: sign-in goes through Odoo's own web page, which returns a one-time
token.

This is a client. It requires the Odoo modules bf_email_management and
bf_sms_archive, which are proprietary (Business Source License 1.1).
