* Email replies: recipients are shown and editable (To, Cc, and Bcc), so is
  the subject, and the original message shows below your text (hide it from
  the ⋮ menu)
* Email: pick the sending address. A reply goes out from the mailbox that
  received the message by default, as on the desktop, with its signature
* Email: bold, italics and lists look the way they will be received, without
  asterisks
* Email: "Undo" for a few seconds after Send (delay in Settings), schedule a
  send, file a new email on a record, discard a draft in place, and recipient
  groups
* Drafts: a "Scheduled" pile, where a send can be held back
* Offline: a queued email keeps its attachments, copies and formatting; if the
  server refuses it, it goes back to Drafts
* Reopening a draft saved on the phone no longer closes the app
