* Pairing a phone is now bound to the app that started it. A custom app scheme
  is not exclusive on Android: another app could declare the same return link,
  receive the pairing code instead of Comms, and trade it for access to your
  messages and your mailbox. An intercepted code is now worth nothing without a
  secret that never leaves this app
* Nothing to redo if your phone is already connected: paired devices keep their
  access and do not go through pairing again
* An updated instance now demands that secret, and refuses a pairing started
  from an older release than this one. An older instance keeps working as before
