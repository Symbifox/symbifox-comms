* Tasks: the list now reads day by day — Overdue, Today, one heading for
  each of the next seven days, then Later. A task due this morning stays in
  Today, and tasks without a deadline come first
* Badges: mail counts unread messages in the inbox, and tasks count what is
  overdue plus what is due today
* Notifications: end-to-end encrypted between your instance and the phone
  once the server is up to date, and the app that delivers them is yours to
  pick (Settings, Notifications) instead of being chosen for you
* Sharing to Comms: a shared file is offered, not sent; it only leaves with
  "Attach" or "Send". Files that are too large are refused before loading
* Security: no session is ever written in clear text when the Android key
  store refuses to open; the app says so, and you will need to sign in again
* Calendar: deadline badges land on the right day when the account's time
  zone differs from the phone's
* New icon, shared by the Symbifox apps: the full-size illustration on a very
  pale blue background, instead of the black disc
* Language: the app follows the phone's, in French or English
