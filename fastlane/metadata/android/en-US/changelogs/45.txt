* Calendar and tasks now refresh on their own: when you come back to the
  screen, then every minute while it is in front. Nothing refreshed them
  before, so a grid opened in the morning still showed the morning at night
* Pull down to refresh, as in messages and mail
* Midnight moves the grid forward: left open, it no longer stays on yesterday
* When a refresh fails, the time of the last successful read is shown rather
  than left to guess
